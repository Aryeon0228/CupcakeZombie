Original procedural UI sounds for CupcakeZombie. No third-party recording.
ui-click.wav: 48 kHz mono 16-bit, 210ms, rounded jelly pop from approved sound concepts.
cupcake-nya.wav: 48 kHz mono 16-bit, 200ms, soft round kitten "nya!" (approved #3 voice, startled and short so drag/click chirps do not pile up). Additive formant synthesis, little energy above 3 kHz. Regenerate: sfx/nya.py (round_ voice, see make step in sfx/README).
Both play through the shared muted/limited audio bus. Drag notes are limited to one per 115ms.
