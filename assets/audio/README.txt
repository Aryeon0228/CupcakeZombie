Original procedural UI sounds for CupcakeZombie. No third-party recording.
ui-click.wav: 48 kHz mono 16-bit, 210ms, rounded jelly pop from approved sound concepts.
cupcake-nya.wav: 48 kHz mono 16-bit, 170ms, synthesized kitten-like nasal-to-open vowel. Regenerate: python3 tools/make-nya-sound.py
Both play through the shared muted/limited audio bus. Drag notes are limited to one per 115ms.
