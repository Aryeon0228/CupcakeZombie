Original procedural UI sounds for CupcakeZombie. No third-party recording.
ui-click.wav: 48 kHz mono 16-bit, 210ms, rounded jelly pop from approved sound concepts.
cupcake-nya.wav: 48 kHz mono 16-bit, 450ms, clean cartoon kitten "nyaa" (approved R2: scoops up ~720->1080 Hz then glides down, 2nd harmonic strongest, no breath). Each new meow cuts the previous with a 25ms fade so drags do not pile up. Regenerate: sfx/nya_ref.py (R2).
Both play through the shared muted/limited audio bus. Drag notes are limited to one per 115ms.
